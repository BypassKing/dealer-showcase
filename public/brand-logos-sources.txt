Included icons: Audi, BMW, Chevrolet, Ford, Honda, Hyundai, Jeep, Kia,
Mazda, MG, Nissan, Peugeot, Renault, Subaru, Tesla, Toyota, Volkswagen, Volvo.
Each SVG was downloaded from that revision's icons/<slug>.svg and given its
Simple Icons brand color as a root fill attribute. Files are same-origin static
assets; the site does not request the source repository at runtime.

Brand names and Chinese translations in brand-logos.js are local text mappings,
not logo assets from Simple Icons. Brands and their marks may remain protected
by trademark and other rights. CC0 applies to the contributed icon artwork;
